\label{app:p1profiles}

$\mathbf{w} = (w_1, w_2, w_3, w_4, w_{\text{probe}})$ of \cref{eq:p1reward}, spanning distinct application
intents. \Cref{tab:app:profiles} lists the six profiles used, together with the label each carries in
\cref{ch:single_path}.

\section{Single-Path Selection: Agent Hyperparameters}
\label{app:p1hyper}

\Cref{tab:app:p1hyper} lists the hyperparameters shared by the flat, scoring, and conditional agents of
\cref{sec:p1impl:agents}; the flat agent differs only in training for 533~episodes rather than 666.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{Hyperparameters for the single-path selection agents (\cref{ch:single_path}).}
  \label{tab:app:p1hyper}
  \begin{tabular}{ll}
    \toprule
    Parameter & Value \\
    \midrule
    Hidden layers / width          & 2 / 128 \\
    Learning rate (Adam)           & $10^{-3}$ \\
    Discount $\gamma$              & 0.95 \\
    Batch size                     & 32 \\
    Replay capacity                & $10^4$ (minimum 500 before learning) \\
    Target update                  & soft (Polyak), $\tau = 0.05$, every 100 gradient steps \\
    Prioritized replay             & $\alpha = 0.6$, $\beta$ annealed $0.4 \rightarrow 1.0$ \\
    Exploration                    & $\varepsilon$-greedy, $1.0 \rightarrow 0.01$, decay 0.995/episode \\
    Dropout                        & 0.1 \\
    Gradient clipping              & 1.0 \\
    Episodes $\times$ steps        & $666 \times 24$ (flat: $533 \times 24$) \\
    Gradient step frequency        & every 4 environment steps \\
    Invalid-action mask value      & $-10^{9}$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}

\section{Multipath System: QoE Weights and SAC Hyperparameters}
\label{app:p2hyper}

The QoE reward of \cref{eq:p2qoe} uses default weights $(a,b,c,d) = (1, 0.5, 0.5, 1)$ with latency and
jitter normalizers of \SI{200}{\milli\second} and \SI{50}{\milli\second}; the application agent acts every
\SI{1}{\second} and the transport agent every frame (${\approx}\SI{33}{\milli\second}$ at 30\,fps) over
a bitrate range of \SIrange{300}{6000}{\kilo\bit\per\second}.
